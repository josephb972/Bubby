Raising your Bubby

Bubby starts as a seed and grows a little every minute the page is open. Keep its hearts full, keep the garden clean and put it to bed on time. How it turns out as an adult depends on how you cared for it as a Bulb and a Bud.

Life cycle

Screen icons

    Good care

    • Answer calls. When the ! icon flashes, Bubby needs something. Ignore it for about 30 seconds and it counts as a care mistake.
    • Meals before snacks. Snacks cheer Bubby up but add weight, and too many in a row cause a toothache.
    • Clean up fast. Poop left on screen makes Bubby sick. Sickness has names: Spore Cough (dirty garden), Itchy Moss (too many sweets or extra weight), Moonflu (sleeping with the lights on) and The Blight (neglect). Each needs its own medicine, sometimes two or three doses. Garden Tea is free but only cures Spore Cough, slowly.
    • Earn points, spend them in the Shop. Points come from opening Bubby each day, caring for it, winning games, daily quests and goals. Spend them on better food, medicine and an Immunity Tonic. Press B for the Shop.
    • Lights off at bedtime. When Bubby falls asleep, press SLEEP. Bright lights keep it up and make it grumpy.
    • Scold tantrums. If Bubby calls while its hearts are full, it is acting out. Use Discipline. Scolding a well-behaved Bubby only upsets it.
    • Neglect has a cost. Leave Bubby starving or sick too long and it packs up and moves away, leaving a new seed behind.
    • Come back daily. Bubby gets a gift each new day and keeps a streak going. Time away makes it a little hungry and sad, but never sick or gone.

    Adult forms

    Bubbydex

    When a Bulb grows into a Bud it becomes a random race. One race is very rare. Raise every race to each adult form to fill the dex.
