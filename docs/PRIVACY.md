# Bubby privacy policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

Bubby is a virtual pet game that runs in your browser as an extension.

**Bubby stores your pet only in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.**

- **What is stored:** your pet's state (its stage, hearts, weight, discipline and recent events) and your Bubby settings
  (pace, bedtime, sound and notification choices). This is saved in your browser's extension storage on your own device.
- **What is collected:** nothing. Bubby has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it makes no
  network requests. No one, including the developer, can see your data.
- **What is shared or sold:** nothing.
- **Notifications** are created by your browser on your device. Their content never leaves your computer.
- **Deleting your data:** removing the extension deletes everything it stored. "Start over" in Bubby's settings resets
  your pet.

### Permissions Bubby uses

- `storage`: to save your pet in your browser.
- `alarms`: to update your pet once a minute while the popup is closed.
- `notifications`: to tell you when your pet needs care. You can turn these off in Bubby's settings.

### Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Bubby will not start collecting
data without updating this policy first.

### Contact

Questions? Use the support tab on Bubby's Chrome Web Store or Edge Add-ons page.
