# Bubby privacy policy

_Effective 5 November 2026_

**Bubby stores your pet only in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.**

Bubby is a virtual pet game that runs as a browser extension.

- **Stored on your device:** your pet (its stage, hearts, weight, discipline and recent events) and your Bubby settings
  (pace, bedtime, look, sound and notifications), in your browser's extension storage.
- **Collected:** nothing. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers, and Bubby makes no network requests.
  Nobody, including the developer, can see your data.
- **Shared or sold:** nothing.
- **Notifications** are shown by your browser on your device; their content never leaves it.
- **Deleting your data:** removing the extension deletes everything it stored. "Start over" in Bubby's settings resets
  your pet.

**Permissions:** `storage` saves your pet, `alarms` updates it once a minute while the popup is closed, and
`notifications` tells you when it needs care (you can turn these off in Bubby's settings).

**Changes:** any change to this policy will be published here with a new date. Bubby will not collect data without
this policy saying so first.

**Contact:** use the support tab on Bubby's Chrome Web Store or Edge Add-ons page.
