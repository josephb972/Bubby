# Bubby shop update: TikTok kit

Files (1080×1920, 30 fps, 22.5 s, audio at -14 LUFS), remade with `node tools/promo-video.mjs`:

- `bubby-shop-tiktok.mp4`: with the original chiptune soundtrack and sound effects. Post this one first: the music
  is ours, so the sound page is a Bubby sound other creators can reuse.
- `bubby-shop-tiktok-sfx-only.mp4`: sound effects only. For a second post with a trending sound added in TikTok's
  editor at about 30–40% volume, so the sad trombone and the buy chime still land.
- `cover.jpg`: the cover frame (the full hook on screen).

Everything in the video is real: the game's own pixel art and animations, and screenshots of the real shop.

Why it's built this way: the full hook is readable on the first frame, there's a relatable joke (kids outgrowing
clothes) with a punchline at 4 s, a satisfying payoff, a fast montage that's worth rewatching, and a question to
answer in the comments. It's under 25 s, so it loops while the viewer reads the comments.

## Posting

- **When:** Tuesday to Thursday, 18:00–21:00 in your audience's time zone. Post once, don't delete and repost.
- **First hour:** reply to every comment. Turn the best "make X next" comment into a reply video (TikTok's "reply
  with video") with that outfit added. It's free content, and the same viewers see it again.
- **Cover:** `cover.jpg`. Keep the on-screen text clear of TikTok's buttons (already done: captions sit between
  y 300 and 1550, nothing important in the right-hand 140 px).
- **Text on the video:** already burned in, so don't add TikTok text stickers on top.
- **Before posting:** the extension must be live in the store (`docs/LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`), or the call to action
  sends people nowhere. If it isn't live yet, post with "coming soon" in the caption and the pinned comment.
