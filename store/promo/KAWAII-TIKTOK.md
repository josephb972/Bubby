# Bubby clothes update: the kawaii TikTok

Made with `node tools/kawaii-video.mjs` (rerun it after any change to the clothes art). 1080×1920, 30 fps, 22.5 s, audio at -14 LUFS.

- `bubby-kawaii-tiktok.mp4`: with the original music-box soundtrack. Post this first, so the sound becomes a "Bubby sound" others can reuse.
- `bubby-kawaii-tiktok-sfx-only.mp4`: only the poofs, chimes and squeaks. For a second post with a trending sound added in TikTok's editor at about 30% volume.
- `cover-kawaii.jpg`: the cover frame (the polaroid reveal).

Everything on screen is the real game: Bubby and every garment come from the game's own art code, and the pod in the outro is a screenshot of the real popup.

## Why this one, and not another Bubby

I dressed all six races in the same outfit and compared them. The **Daisy Leafling** (white petals, yellow face) wins: it reads as "cute" in half a second, it has a big face for the heart glasses, and white petals let every pastel pop. Its name in the video is "Mochi".

## Posting

- **When:** Tuesday to Thursday, 18:00–21:00 in your audience's time zone. Post once.
- **First hour:** reply to every comment. Turn the best "make it wear X" comment into a reply video. Two outfits are not in the game yet (wizard hat and overalls were not used in this video), so use those if people ask.
- **Safe zones:** captions stay between y 300 and 1560 and clear of the right-hand buttons. Don't add TikTok text stickers on top.
- **Before posting:** the "free · Chrome + Android" line must be true. The Android app is a test build (APK) until it is on Google Play, so if people ask where to get it, point them to the Chrome Web Store and say Android is coming. Edit the end card in `tools/kawaii-video.mjs` if you prefer "free on Chrome".
