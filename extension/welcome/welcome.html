Hello from Bubby!

A seed has been planted in your toolbar. It will sprout in about a minute.

  1. Pin Bubby to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle-piece icon at the top right of the browser, then the pin next to Bubby. Now you can see Bubby's face all the time.

  2. Glance at the icon

    Bubby's face shows its mood. A red ! badge means it is calling you, z means it's asleep.

  3. Click to take care of it

    The toy opens right in your toolbar. When the ! tile flashes, tap it to see what Bubby needs, with a button that does it. Or press Alt + Shift + B from any tab.

  4. Set Bubby's bedtime

    When do you go to bed? Bubby sleeps when you do, and won't send notifications at night. You can change this later under the ⚙ gear in the popup.

Bubby lives in real time, even while the browser is closed (Chill pace: about 10 days per life). Want to see everything quickly? Switch to the Quick pace in settings for the original 20-minute life.
