Raising your Bubby

Bubby starts as a seed and grows a little every minute the page is open. Keep its hearts full, keep the garden clean and put it to bed on time. How it turns out as an adult depends on how you cared for it as a Bulb and a Bud.

Life cycle

Screen icons

    Good care

    • Answer calls. When the ! icon flashes, Bubby needs something. Ignore it for about 30 seconds and it counts as a care mistake.
    • Meals before snacks. Snacks cheer Bubby up but add weight, and too many in a row cause a toothache.
    • Clean up fast. Poop left on screen makes Bubby sick. Sick Bubbies need medicine, sometimes two or three doses.
    • Lights off at bedtime. When Bubby falls asleep, press SLEEP. Bright lights keep it up and make it grumpy.
    • Scold tantrums. If Bubby calls while its hearts are full, it is acting out. Use Discipline. Scolding a well-behaved Bubby only upsets it.
    • Neglect has a cost. Leave Bubby starving or sick too long and it packs up and moves away, leaving a new seed behind.
